Stoney Lake Island Bunkie

A Space to Grow, Gather & Unwind

As the family grew, so did the need for additional space at their island cottage on beautiful Stoney Lake. The goal was to create a separate retreat where the kids could spend time with friends, enjoy a little independence, and provide comfortable overnight accommodations for visiting guests.

Built on a durable steel foundation, this custom bunkie features Hardie cement board siding for long-lasting protection against the elements. Inside, engineered hardwood flooring and MDF wall panels with decorative battens create a warm, inviting atmosphere with a clean, timeless finish.

Surrounded by panoramic views of Stoney Lake, this thoughtfully designed bunkie provides the perfect balance of comfort, functionality, and cottage charm; creating a space for new memories while giving everyone a little more room to enjoy island life.
